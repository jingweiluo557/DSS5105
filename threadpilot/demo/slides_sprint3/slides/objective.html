Who it serves

The general manager of a quick-response knitwear factory: 120 orders, 34 in progress, four stages, eight outside workshops. On the floor, not at a desk; no SQL.

Why it matters

Each question costs a call to a planner and a wait — about 73 planner-hours a month by our Sprint 1 estimate — and a wrong answer costs a missed delivery: 8 of the 34 open orders are already late.

ThreadPilot

Answers from the factory’s own records with the rows behind them, estimates new orders, and acts — chase-ups, reminders — only after the manager confirms.
