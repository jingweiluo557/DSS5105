What we achieved, and what we learned

Achieved

A co-pilot a manager can use all day: briefing, answers with their source rows, expedite and feasibility simulations, chase-ups drafted before she asks, watches, alert emails and voice input.

69% on unseen questions (95% CI 57–78%); 10/10 on production and normal-range checks; never an email without a confirmed preview.

It says “not in the records” instead of inventing an order, a stage or a number.

Learned

A score on questions you have tuned against is a regression check, not accuracy: fresh questions dropped us from 100% to 69%.

Rules belong in code, not in the prompt: the model reads and words; the tools compute and gate.

The misses cluster: build the missing tool before polishing the prompt.

Thank you — questions welcome.
